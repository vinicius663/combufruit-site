Born on Combu Island

The fourth largest of the 39 islands surrounding Belém gave our company its name and still defines the way we work: close to the fruit, close to the people who harvest it.

From the islands region to the world

It is on Combu Island, among managed native açaí palms, that growers harvest the açaí that feeds our production — and it is there that the negotiation happens, harvest after harvest.

This closeness is not a marketing detail: it is what guarantees fruit at the right standard, traceability from harvest onward and direct income for the island's families.

From the island, the açaí goes to the processing plant and from there to industries and distributors in Brazil and abroad.
